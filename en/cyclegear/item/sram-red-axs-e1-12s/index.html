Verified Incompatibility & Clearance Notes

  • STRICTLY INCOMPATIBLE with Shimano HG / HG-L2 freehubs and Shimano 12-speed Hyperglide+ chains/cassettes — requires SRAM XDR freehub body and SRAM 12-speed Flattop chain.
  • Requires a SRAM DUB (28.99 mm) bottom bracket — Shimano 24 mm Hollowtech II bottom brackets will NOT accept a DUB crank spindle.
  • RED E1 integrated Quarq power meter chainrings are one-piece direct-mount units (non-replaceable individual rings when worn, though SRAM offers a trade-in replacement discount).

Before You Buy — Real-World Edge Cases & Local Gotchas

  • Because front and rear derailleurs share identical snap-off batteries (~60 hrs), a dead rear battery mid-ride can be swapped with the front derailleur battery in 10 seconds to ride home in the small or big ring.
  • Red E1's 2,496 g official weight INCLUDES the integrated dual-sided Quarq spider power meter — making it lighter than Dura-Ace R9270 once a power meter is added to Shimano.
  • Switching from Shimano to SRAM RED AXS E1 carries hidden ecosystem costs: you must swap wheelset and smart-trainer freehubs to SRAM XDR (unless using Zwift Cog) and install a DUB bottom bracket.

What mount/BB/freehub standards and drivetrains are compatible? +

Mount/BB standard: SRAM DUB 28.99 mm spindle (BSA DUB, BB86/PF86 DUB, T47 DUB, BB30/PF30 DUB) + Flat-Mount Disc; Freehub/cleat interface: SRAM XDR 12-speed road freehub body ONLY (incompatible with Shimano HG / HG-L2). What are the hookless rim limits and torque safety requirements? +

Torque the SRAM DUB self-extracting crank bolt to 54 Nm (8 mm hex), XG-1290 XDR cassette to 40 Nm, and Centerlock rotor lockrings to 40 Nm. Use ONLY DOT 5.1 brake fluid (NEVER Shimano Mineral Oil) and avoid skin/paint contact when bleeding.

What are the official weight, wireless protocols, and head-unit integrations? +

Official weight is 2,496 g, battery life 60 hr. Connectivity: SRAM AXS Proprietary Wireless, ANT+, Bluetooth Low Energy (AXS App). Verified ecosystems: Hammerhead Karoo (deepest native SRAM AXS integration), Garmin Edge 840 Solar & Edge 1050 (gear ratio + Bonus Button control), COROS DURA (gear ratio + Bonus Button ANT+ control), Wahoo ELEMNT ROAM V2 (gear ratio + Bonus Button mapping).

Mandatory Cycling Safety: Hookless 5.0 bar / 72.5 psi Cap & Torque Spec

Never exceed the ISO/ETRTO hookless (TSS) maximum inflation ceiling of 5.0 bar (72.5 psi), and always verify your exact tire-rim pairing on both wheel and tire manufacturer approved lists before riding. Install power meters, pedals, and drivetrain components using a calibrated torque wrench per manufacturer manuals.
