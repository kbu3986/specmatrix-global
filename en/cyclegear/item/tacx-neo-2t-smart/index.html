Verified Incompatibility & Clearance Notes

  • Long-cage rear derailleurs or oversized pulley wheel systems (OSPW) may come close to the lower flywheel housing in the largest cog — verify cage clearance before pedaling under load.
  • Must install Tacx firmware v2.6.0+ via the Tacx Training App and connect to Zwift over Bluetooth (not ANT+) to unlock Zwift Virtual Shifting and 10 Hz Race Mode.
  • Requires Garmin Tacx Type-2 (12 mm axle) XDR freehub body (T2875-01); legacy NEO 1 / NEO 2 Type-1 (9 mm axle) freehub bodies do NOT fit NEO 2T.

Before You Buy — Real-World Edge Cases & Local Gotchas

  • Zero belts and zero physical flywheel mean virtually zero mechanical whine (~52 dBA @ 30 km/h, chain-noise limited) and zero spindown calibration drift across room temperature swings.
  • Can run completely unplugged at race warmups (powering Bluetooth/ANT+ from your pedaling), though downhill drive and road-feel vibration require AC mains power.
  • Ships without a cassette (except dedicated Zwift Cog bundles) and lacks built-in Wi-Fi/Ethernet unless stepping up to the pricier NEO 3M with network adapter.

Quick Engineering Q&A (Verified Specs)

SSOT Derived
What mount/BB/freehub standards and drivetrains are compatible? +

Mount/BB standard: Direct-drive rear dropout mount (130/135 mm QR, 12×142 mm road disc, 12×148 mm Boost Thru-Axle); Freehub/cleat interface: Shimano/SRAM 8–12s HG freehub default (Tacx Type 2 XDR/XD & Campagnolo bodies optional; Zwift Cog compatible on stock HG body). Verified compatible drivetrains: Shimano Dura-Ace R9270 / Ultegra R8170 / 105 R7170 12s (direct fit on stock HG freehub), Shimano 8/9/10/11s HG cassettes, SRAM 12s AXS (with Garmin Tacx XDR Type-2 freehub T2875-01 OR Zwift Cog virtual shifting), Campagnolo 11s/12s (with optional Campagnolo body or Zwift Cog).

What are the hookless rim limits and torque safety requirements? +

Use the included 2.7 mm disc brake caliper spacer if your flat-mount caliper contacts the NEO 2T disk housing, torque the cassette lockring to 40 Nm, and secure the rear thru-axle to frame spec (10–12 Nm). Lift using the built-in chassis recess (21.5 kg) with the legs locked before riding.

What are the official weight, wireless protocols, and head-unit integrations? +

Official weight is 21,500 g, claimed accuracy ±1.0%, max resistance 2,200 W. Connectivity: ANT+ FE-C, Bluetooth FTMS, 10 Hz Race Mode (firmware 2.6.0+). Verified ecosystems: Zwift (Virtual Shifting + Road Feel cobblestone/gravel + 10 Hz Race Mode), Garmin Tacx Training App / TrainerRoad / Rouvy / MyWhoosh, Garmin Edge 840 Solar & Edge 1050 (native Tacx workout control), COROS DURA (ANT+ FE-C / BLE FTMS).

Mandatory Cycling Safety: Hookless 5.0 bar / 72.5 psi Cap & Torque Spec

Never exceed the ISO/ETRTO hookless (TSS) maximum inflation ceiling of 5.0 bar (72.5 psi), and always verify your exact tire-rim pairing on both wheel and tire manufacturer approved lists before riding. Install power meters, pedals, and drivetrain components using a calibrated torque wrench per manufacturer manuals.

Use the included 2.7 mm disc brake caliper spacer if your flat-mount caliper contacts the NEO 2T disk housing, torque the cassette lockring to 40 Nm, and secure the rear thru-axle to frame spec (10–12 Nm). Lift using the built-in chassis recess (21.5 kg) with the legs locked before riding.
