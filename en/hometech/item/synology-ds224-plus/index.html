Before You Buy — Real-World Edge Cases & Local Gotchas

  • No internal M.2 NVMe cache slots and dual 1GbE NICs cap sequential single-client transfers at ~112 MB/s (no built-in 2.5GbE without unofficial USB adapters).
  • Default 2 GB soldered DDR4 RAM fills quickly once Container Manager (Docker) or Synology Photos AI indexing runs — budget an extra $15–$25 for a compatible 4 GB (or community-tested 8 GB/16 GB) DDR4 SODIMM.
  • Official 14.69 W access wattage was measured with two low-power 1 TB drives; high-capacity 7200 RPM helium NAS HDDs (12–18 TB) add roughly 6–10 W under active load.
