Endurance Energy Gels Matrix: True Cost per 30 g Carbs, Ratios & 90 g/hr Audit

Side-by-side label audit of 11 verified marathon and cycling gels ranked by pure carbohydrate unit cost, glucose:fructose ratio, caffeine, sodium, and pocket weight.

Per-packet pricing hides massive gaps in carbohydrate payload—from 19 g in GU Roctane to 90 g in Precision PF 90. This evidence-anchored matrix normalizes every verified energy gel to a standardized 30 g pure-carbohydrate benchmark and calculates exact packet counts and hourly fueling budgets for 60, 90, and 120 g/hr race protocols.

04. Only Neversecond C30 (200 mg) and GU Roctane (180 mg) deliver meaningful built-in sodium; Maurten, SiS, and Precision PF gels require separate electrolyte pairing.
