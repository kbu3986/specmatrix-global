MacBook Pro 16-Inch (140 W PD 3.1 EPR) Compatible Power Banks & GaN Chargers

Verified 28 V / 5 A (140 W) USB-PD 3.1 compatibility guide separating true 140 W fast-charge bricks & flight-safe packs from 100 W PD 3.0 bottlenecks.

Fast-charging a 16-inch MacBook Pro (M1/M2/M3/M4 Pro & Max, 100 Wh battery) from 0 to 50% in ~30 minutes requires true USB-PD 3.1 EPR (28 V / 5 A = 140 W) plus a 5 A MagSafe 3 or 240 W E-Marker USB-C cable. This compatibility matrix verifies all 10 true-140 W power banks and GaN chargers in our database—including which models KEEP 140 W active when you plug in a second device.

CHECKPOINT #1

Only 5 power banks and 5 GaN chargers in Wave 1 deliver genuine 140 W PD 3.1 EPR—models like Satechi 165 W, Shargeek 140, and Anker Prime 200 W cap single-port output at 100 W.

CHECKPOINT #2

For keeping 140 W active WHILE charging a second device: choose Anker Prime 27,650 mAh (140+100 W), CUKTECH 20 (140+60 W), Anker Prime 250 W (140+100 W), or UGREEN Nexode 300 W (140+100+60 W).

CHECKPOINT #3

On 100 Wh MacBook Pro 16 batteries, a flight-legal 99.54 Wh Anker Prime delivers ~0.82 real full charges (81.6 Wh usable), while 86.4 Wh packs deliver ~0.70 full charges.

Compatibility Verification Protocol

Compatibility requires verified 28 V / 5 A EPR output on at least one USB-C port. Multi-port retention flags check whether USB-C1 remains at 140 W during 2-port or 3-port simultaneous loads.
