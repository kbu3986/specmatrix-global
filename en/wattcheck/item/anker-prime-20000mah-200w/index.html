Before You Buy — Real-World Edge Cases & Local Gotchas

  • No USB-PD 3.1 140 W output; single-port ceiling is 100 W (20 V / 5 A), so a 16-inch MacBook Pro charges at 100 W instead of 140 W.
  • Early production batches of A1336 cap PPS at 3.3-11 V / 3 A (Samsung 25 W), whereas later hardware revisions support 5 A PPS (45 W); check box label before relying on SFC 2.0.
  • Lacks Bluetooth app control unlike the larger A1340 27,650 mAh model.
