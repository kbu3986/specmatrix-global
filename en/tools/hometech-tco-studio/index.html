Electrical Safety, 220V Mains, Active PFC UPS & Electricity TCO Notice

Always verify regional mains voltage, grounding plug type (Type C/F for Korea 220V/60Hz), and local electrical safety certifications before operating imported power hardware. For NAS, Mini PCs, and workstations with Active PFC power supplies, a Pure Sine Wave UPS is strongly recommended because stepped/simulated-sine UPS units can cause transfer dropouts under load. Electricity and 3-year TCO outputs are deterministic estimates using baseline tariffs (US $0.17/kWh, KR KEPCO Tier 2 ₩214/kWh, DE €0.38/kWh) and vary with seasonal progressive tiers, duty cycle, and HDD spin-down settings.
